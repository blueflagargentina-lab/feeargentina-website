## A Historic Milestone for Chinese Beaches

Weihai International Bathing Beach, located in Shandong Province, has become the first beach in China to receive the Blue Flag eco-certification. The award ceremony, held in May, was organized by the Center for Environmental Education and Communications (CEEC) of the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, which serves as the National Operator of the Blue Flag Programme in China.

## From Beautiful Bays to Blue Flag Standards

Established in 1992, the beach forms part of Weihai Bay and was previously recognized under China's national "Beautiful Bays" initiative, a coastal restoration program launched in 2021. The roughly 2,800-meter beach draws around 8 million visitors annually, with the certified stretch covering approximately 1,600 meters of consistently high water quality. According to Blue Flag International's CEO, Daniel Schaffer, the achievement reflects strong alignment between China's domestic bay-restoration efforts and international Blue Flag standards.

## Local Investment and Community Engagement

Since 2018, local authorities in Weihai's Torch High-Tech Industrial Development Zone have invested more than 300 million yuan in ecological restoration projects, including the remediation of wastewater outlets, helping maintain 100% excellent water quality in nearby coastal areas. Ahead of certification, the beach underwent upgrades guided by CEEC and the Ocean University of China, including improved multilingual signage, accessibility features, and biodiversity education displays. Since January 2026, volunteer groups have organized dozens of cleanup events, engaging hundreds of residents and tourists in conservation activities.

## Looking Ahead to 2026 and Beyond

City officials, including Vice Mayor Liang Hao, described the certification as both an honor and a responsibility, pledging to develop a replicable "Weihai model" for sustainable beach management. Following a domestic review in February 2026 and international assessment in April 2026, the beach's certification is expected to serve as a template for future Blue Flag applications across China's coastal regions.
