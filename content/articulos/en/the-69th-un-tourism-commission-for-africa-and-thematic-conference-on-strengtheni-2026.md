## Regional Meeting to Address Tourism Workforce Challenges

The Government of the Republic of Seychelles, in partnership with UN Tourism, will host the 69th Meeting of the UN Tourism Regional Commission for Africa in Victoria, Mahé, from July 2 to 4, 2026. The gathering will be followed by a thematic conference titled "Strengthening Human Capital to Boost Africa's Tourism Growth," bringing together government representatives, industry leaders and tourism professionals from across the continent.

## A Focus on Skills and Sustainable Coastal Development

Organizers describe the event as a strategic platform to advance tourism management across Africa, with particular attention to education and workforce development. For island nations like Seychelles, whose economy depends heavily on coastal and marine tourism, building a skilled, competitive workforce is considered essential to maintaining high environmental and service standards at beaches, resorts and marinas that attract international visitors.

## Agenda Highlights

The provisional agenda includes reports from the UN Tourism Secretary-General and Regional Director for Africa, along with findings from a continent-wide survey on access to tourism education and human capital development. A dedicated session will also present a case study on Seychelles' own tourism human resources development strategy, offering insights that could inform sustainable tourism planning in other coastal destinations throughout 2026 and beyond.

## Broader Implications for Coastal Tourism

By linking workforce training to sustainable growth, the conference aims to position tourism as a key driver of economic development across Africa while reinforcing responsible management of coastal and marine resources. Delegates are expected to discuss best practices that support environmentally conscious tourism operations, a theme increasingly relevant for destinations seeking international recognition for sustainable beach and marina management.
