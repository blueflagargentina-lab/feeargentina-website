## A Continental Gathering on Food and Travel

The Government of Sierra Leone will partner with UN Tourism to host the third edition of the Regional Forum on Gastronomy Tourism for Africa, scheduled for 2-4 December 2026 in Lungi. The event follows previous gatherings held in Zimbabwe in 2024 and Tanzania in 2025, continuing a series designed to strengthen the role of culinary traditions in national and regional tourism strategies across the continent.

## Linking Culture, Communities and Coastal Economies

This year's forum will bring together policymakers, destination managers and international experts to examine how gastronomy tourism can support local communities, protect cultural heritage and contribute to environmental resilience. Sessions will address themes ranging from intellectual property protections for regional food products to the role of women across the gastronomy value chain, with particular attention to barriers faced by women-led enterprises in accessing finance and formal markets.

## Agritourism and Rural-Coastal Connections

A dedicated panel will explore agritourism as a bridge between agriculture, culture and travel, highlighting how traditional food practices and farming activities can be transformed into sustainable tourism products that diversify rural economies. Organizers note that such models can also reinforce coastal and nature-based tourism offerings, since many destinations combine agricultural hinterlands with beach and marine attractions that benefit from coordinated sustainability planning.

## Technical Tours and On-the-Ground Experiences

On the forum's closing day, international delegates will take part in full-day technical tours featuring historic, leisure and nature-based itineraries, alongside beach entertainment and a street food tasting festival. These activities are intended to showcase Sierra Leone's tourism assets while reinforcing the forum's broader goal: positioning African destinations, including coastal communities, as competitive, sustainable players in the global tourism landscape ahead of 2026 and beyond.

## Registration and Participation

The deadline for registration has been extended to 15 November 2026, with workshops open to accredited representatives of UN Tourism Member States and partner organizations. The working language for technical sessions is English, with interpretation into French available during the high-level panels.
