## National Ceremony Highlights Türkiye's Coastal Achievements

Türkiye's 2026 Blue Flag Award Ceremony took place at the Yenifoça Oasis Marina in Foça, İzmir, following the Foundation for Environmental Education's (FEE) global announcement of this year's certifications. The event celebrated the country's beaches, marinas, tourist boats, and private yachts that met the strict environmental and safety standards required for the internationally recognized eco-label.

## Record Numbers Reinforce Global Standing

For 2026, Türkiye earned Blue Flag certification for 580 beaches, 30 marinas, 18 tourist boats, and 26 private yachts, keeping its position as the third-ranked country worldwide for Blue Flag beaches. The ceremony drew a wide range of officials, including İzmir Governor Süleyman Elban, TÜRÇEV Chairman and former Tourism Minister Abdulkadir Ateş, several district governors and mayors from Foça, Fethiye, and Didim, along with members of the National Blue Flag Jury and representatives from the tourism sector.

## A Milestone for Marina and Yacht Certification

The event featured the raising of the Blue Flag alongside a performance by students from Yeni Foça Kindergarten, an Eco-Schools participant. In a notable first for the country, Oasis Marina and seven affiliated private yachts simultaneously received Blue Flag status, while the on-site Oasis Houses Hotel was awarded the International Green Key certification—marking the first instance in Türkiye of a marina, its yachts, and an associated hotel all achieving international environmental recognition together.

## Sustained Commitment to Environmental Education

Speaking at the ceremony, Ateş stressed that maintaining Blue Flag status requires continuous effort, noting that while earning the award is difficult, losing it can happen quickly without sustained environmental commitment. The event also recognized municipalities, hotels, and marina operators for outstanding environmental education initiatives, with Fethiye Municipality ranking third among best practices from 54 countries in FEE's international evaluation, a distinction presented to Mayor Alim Karaca.

## Long-Standing Programme Continues to Grow

Coordinated by TÜRÇEV since 1993, the Blue Flag programme evaluates water quality, environmental management, safety, accessibility, and educational outreach. The 2026 results reaffirm Türkiye's ongoing investment in sustainable coastal tourism and its efforts to align local practices with international environmental standards.
