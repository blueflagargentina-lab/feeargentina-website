## Blue Dialogue Brings Together International Stakeholders

Ahead of the COP31 climate summit, representatives from Türkiye's Environmental Foundation (TÜRÇEV) and the Portuguese Environmental Education and Blue Flag Association (ABAAE) gathered in Trabzon for the "Ocean & Seas: Climate Change and Resilience Blue Dialogue." TÜRÇEV President Abdulkadir Ateş and General Manager Almıla Kından Cebbari led the Turkish delegation, joined by ABAAE President José Archer, representing the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE), the global body overseeing the Blue Flag certification programme.

## Marine Ecosystems and Climate Finance on the Table

The gathering focused on several pressing issues affecting the world's oceans, including the deterioration of marine ecosystems due to climate change, funding mechanisms for ocean conservation projects, and strategies to restore damaged coastal habitats. Participants also examined how marine areas can build greater resilience against rising temperatures, acidification, and extreme weather events linked to climate change.

## International Marine Litter Platform Showcased

During the event, delegates were introduced to the International Marine Litter Platform Initiative, a project coordinated by Türkiye's General Directorate of Environmental Management under the Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change. TÜRÇEV has contributed to this initiative through its work with the Blue Flag Programme, reinforcing the link between local certification efforts and broader international environmental policy.

## Oceans Take Center Stage at COP31

The Trabzon meeting forms part of a broader push to elevate ocean and sea issues within the COP31 agenda, marking the 31st Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change. A series of preparatory workshops and consultations have been organized to ensure marine protection, climate financing, and ecosystem restoration receive high-level attention during the summit and its accompanying Leaders' Summit, scheduled to take place in Antalya from 9 to 20 November 2026.

## Continued Commitment to Coastal Protection

TÜRÇEV has indicated it will continue supporting the protection of marine and coastal ecosystems through environmental education programmes and policy development, working alongside national and international partners. The organization's involvement in both the Blue Flag Programme and preparatory COP31 discussions underscores the growing intersection between local certification schemes and global climate diplomacy.
