## A Global Push for Responsible Travel

UN Tourism, the United Nations agency overseeing global tourism policy, has confirmed plans for the official opening ceremony of the International Year of Sustainable and Resilient Tourism 2027. The face-to-face event, open to the public, will be coordinated by the organization's Communications Department and is expected to bring together government representatives, industry leaders and sustainability advocates from across all regions.

Registration for the ceremony is being managed through UN Tourism's digital platform, with sign-ups accepted until late November 2026. While a detailed program has not yet been released, organizers describe the gathering as a milestone moment meant to set the tone for a full year of coordinated action on sustainable and resilient tourism practices worldwide.

## Relevance for Coastal and Marine Destinations

Although the initiative covers tourism broadly, coastal regions are expected to feature prominently in the discussions leading into 2027. Beaches, marinas and other waterfront destinations remain among the most visited—and most environmentally sensitive—tourism assets globally. Programs such as Blue Flag, which certifies beaches and marinas meeting strict environmental, safety and accessibility standards, are frequently cited as models for the kind of sustainable management the UN initiative hopes to promote more broadly.

## Looking Ahead to 2027

As preparations continue through 2026, stakeholders in coastal tourism—including local governments, marina operators and environmental certification bodies—are likely to align their own sustainability agendas with the UN's designated year. For destinations already pursuing Blue Flag certification or similar eco-labels, the International Year of Sustainable and Resilient Tourism 2027 may offer additional visibility and policy support, reinforcing the global push toward cleaner beaches, better-managed marinas and more resilient coastal economies.

Further details on the opening ceremony's agenda, speakers and regional participation are expected to be released by UN Tourism in the coming months.
