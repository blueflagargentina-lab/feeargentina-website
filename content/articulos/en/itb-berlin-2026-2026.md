## A Global Platform for Sustainable Tourism
ITB Berlin 2026, scheduled to take place from March 2 to 5, 2026, will once again serve as one of the world's leading gatherings for the tourism industry. Organized in partnership with UN Tourism and its affiliate member Messe Berlin, the event will bring together government ministers, private sector leaders, and destination managers to address how tourism growth can be made more inclusive, resilient, and environmentally responsible.

## Coastal Destinations Under the Spotlight
Among the key sessions planned for the fair is the UN Tourism Ministers' Summit, themed "Empowering Emerging Destinations: From Potential to Performance." The summit will explore practical strategies for managing tourism expansion, a conversation that carries direct relevance for coastal regions and beach destinations seeking to balance visitor numbers with environmental protection. Certification programs such as Blue Flag, which recognize beaches and marinas meeting strict standards for water quality, safety, and environmental management, are increasingly cited by destination managers as practical tools to achieve this balance.

## Roundtable to Address Tourism's Environmental Role
A central highlight of ITB Berlin 2026 will be "The Global Roundtable: Leading Tourism into Balance," set for March 3. The session will bring together stakeholders to discuss how destinations can harmonize economic growth with sustainability and community well-being. Organizers have indicated that tourism's role in fostering resilience amid environmental and social pressures will be a core theme, a discussion closely tied to the broader push for certified sustainable practices along coastlines and in marine tourism infrastructure.

## Looking Ahead
The event will also feature the launch of the Tourism for Rural Development Small Grants Programme and the Colourful Cultures Africa initiative, supported by the TUI Care Foundation, alongside a Leaders Dinner addressing investment opportunities in Angola's tourism sector. As the industry prepares for the 2026 edition of ITB Berlin, coastal sustainability and eco-certification schemes like Blue Flag are expected to remain prominent reference points in discussions about the future direction of global tourism.
